How It Works

Exchange your books with local readers through a simple and organized process.

1

Create Your Account

Register using your name, email, phone number, location and password. After registration you can access your personal dashboard.

2

Add Your Books

Add books that you are willing to exchange. Enter the title, author, category, condition, language, description and an optional cover image.

3

Browse Other Books

Search available books by title, author, category, condition and location.

4

Send an Exchange Request

Select the book you want and offer one of your own available books in return.

5

Owner Accepts or Rejects

The owner can review the request and either accept or reject your proposed exchange.

6

Communicate

Use the messaging feature to communicate with the other user regarding the exchange.

7

Complete the Exchange

Once both users have exchanged their books, the exchange can be marked as completed.
