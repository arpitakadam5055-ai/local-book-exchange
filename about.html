About Local Book Exchange

Connecting readers and giving books a second life within local communities.

Our Idea


Many readers have books that remain unused after they finish reading them. At the same time, other readers may be searching for exactly those books.


Local Book Exchange provides a simple platform where users can list their books, discover books owned by other users and request a direct exchange.

Our Goal


The goal of the project is to create a community-based book sharing platform that makes books easier to discover and encourages reuse.


Users can manage their own books, maintain a wishlist, communicate with other readers and track exchange requests from one dashboard.

What the Platform Provides

Features designed for a simple book exchange experience.

📖

Book Listing

Users can add books with title, author, category, condition and description.

🔎

Book Discovery

Search and filter available books according to your reading interests.

🔁

Exchange Requests

Offer one of your books in exchange for a book listed by another user.

❤️

Wishlist

Save interesting books so you can easily find them again later.

💬

Messaging

Communicate with other users about the details of a book exchange.

🛡️

Administration

Administrators can manage users, books, categories, exchanges and reports.
